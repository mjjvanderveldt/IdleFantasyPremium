Depending on the difference in tier between the {tool_type} used for {verb}, and the level required for {verb} {activity_name}, you can receive an additional mult to your standard tool efficiency. The effect of this is that when you're {verb} lower tier {activity_name} your tool has a much higher efficiency with all the bonuses that entails.

For normal tools, their tier is determined by their minimum level required to equip. For the {heirloom_tool}, its tier is defined by your current {skill_name} level. The heirloom's tool efficiency itself is calculated separately as described in {heirloom_link}.

For {activity_name}, the tier depends on the minimum required level for {verb} the relevant {activity_name}.
