# {icon} Fishing

Catch fish and aquatic treasures from the waters.

## Fishing drops

When fishing, for each frame (of which there are 60 frames per session), the drops you receive depend on a random roll. 80% of the time, you will receive the fish that you initially selected with the quantity multiplied by the tool efficiency. 20% of the time, you'll receive random drops from the rare items table.

XP is always calculated as $`selected\ fish\ XP \times tool\ efficiency \times pet\ boosts`$ independent of drops.

### Rare items

Whenever you roll to receive items from the rare items table, each item will roll independently so at most, you could receive all items for your fishing level. If you receive no rare items, you will instead get the standard amount of fish as per the standard roll.
