# {icon} Farming

Plant seeds in up to **8 patches** and return after the growth time to harvest. A {garden_link} building adds up to 2 more patches and the **Grand Monument** provides another additional patch

{seed_table}

Seeds can be purchased from the **Shop** under Seeds & Farming or by {thieving_link} them from NPCs.

## Hoes

Equip a hoe to increase your harvest yield. The bonus is applied on top of the base yield.

## 🫘 The Magic Bean

Deep in the soil of Gielinor, something stirs.

Farmers whisper of a rare, unusual seed -- unlike any other. It has been known to appear, once in a great while, as a surprise among a harvest. Some say it responds to the earth differently. Some say it grows... impossibly tall.

Its true nature is unclear. Those who have found one speak only in riddles.
