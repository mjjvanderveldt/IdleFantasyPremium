# {icon} Firemaking

Burn logs from your inventory to gain Firemaking XP. Each log burned produces **ashes** which can be scattered for bonus Prayer XP and can also be used for bonuses in {farming_link}, {herblore_link}, and {runecrafting_link}.

{item_table}

## Tinderboxes

Equip a tinderbox to multiply how many logs you can burn per session. Higher-tier tinderboxes require a higher Firemaking level.
