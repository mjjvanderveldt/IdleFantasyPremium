Rune Essence can be mined or purchased from the **Shop**.

## Bonus crafted runes

When producing runes, you can receive bonus runes from your Runecrafting level and any included ash catalysts.

For the level bonuses, at level 50+ you produce 1 additional rune per essence; at level 75+ you produce an additional 2 runes. For ashes, the number of bonus runes depends on the ash's tier.
