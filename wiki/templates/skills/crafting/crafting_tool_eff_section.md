Depending on the difference in tier between the tool used for {verb} and the item you are {verb}, you can receive an additional mult to your standard tool efficiency. The effect of this is that you can craft items with lower tiers potentially much faster than items with higher tiers if you have higher-tiered tools.

For normal tools, their tier is determined by their minimum level required to equip. For the {heirloom_tool}, its tier is defined by your current {skill_name} level. The heirloom's tool efficiency itself is calculated separately as described in {heirloom_link}.

For items, their tier depends on the minimum required level for {verb}.
