# Isle Dungeons & Bosses

The isle has **4 dungeons** and **2 raid bosses**. The 4 dungeons feed the story chain and the Ancient Sigil economy. The two bosses bookend the isle: one is the unlock, the other is the finale.

## Dungeons

Each isle dungeon has a two-enemy roster and a guaranteed Ancient Sigil rare-drop table that scales with the tier (one guaranteed sigil at Beach & Cliffs, three per clear at Abyssal Depths). All 4 dungeons show up on the isle Combat tab; mainland dungeons are filtered out entirely.

## Bosses

{boss_table}

### {sea_serpent_name}

The unlock. Available from the Dock as the Voyage quest once you reach Construction 90 and finish building the Dock. Bring an endgame combat kit, full food, and a spell/arrow loadout you trust; the fight is designed to be beatable but not a formality. Defeating the Sea Serpent once permanently enables Set Sail from the mainland.

The Sea Serpent has its own boss page with the full stat block, roster, and loot.

### {last_elder_name}

The finale. Gated behind the full 8-piece Elder set: the fight will not open on the isle Combat tab until you own every Elder piece. The Last Elder has the isle's rarest drop table, including the {ancient_signet_link} accessory (guaranteed on first kill), a chance at the ultra-rare **Elder Familiar** pet, and additional Ancient Sigils for repeat clears.

Defeating the Last Elder the first time also completes the last story quest and awards the Isle Champion title.
